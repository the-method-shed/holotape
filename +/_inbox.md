# Inbox

Use this inbox for an unfinished thought, a source link, or a clipping you want to revisit. Say where it came from, why you saved it, and what is still uncertain; a link and a few words can be enough. Do not put secrets, sensitive attachments, or raw private data here.

## Let it cool, then review

Treat `+/` as a place to let new material sit before deciding what matters, not a queue that must be filed immediately. This approach is adapted from Ideaverse Lite 1.5's *Add* note (a local reference vault); its content is not imported here. Revisit items when ready rather than applying a fixed waiting period:

1. Check the source and why the item was saved. Add context, a useful link, or a question if it helps make the item worth revisiting.
2. If it remains useful, review its provenance, rights, sensitivity, and destination with a human before linking it from a [project](../Projects/_projects.md) or [map](../Atlas/Maps/Maps.md), or turning it into a durable [note](../Atlas/Notes/_notes.md). Nothing here is automatically approved team knowledge.
3. If it has no lasting value, ask before discarding someone else's capture or a source record. Remove its [index](../index.md) entry when deleting it; don't silently erase evidence for a claim already used elsewhere.

This page describes the folder; it is not an inbox item. The view shows the 10 newest inbox items by file creation time, with their age in days. On GitHub, [browse the inbox folder](./) to see every item.
