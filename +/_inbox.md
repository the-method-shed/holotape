# Inbox

Use this inbox for an unfinished thought, a source link, or a clipping you want to revisit. Say where it came from, why you saved it, and what is still uncertain; a link and a few words can be enough. Do not put secrets, sensitive attachments, or raw private data here.

Nothing here is automatically approved team knowledge. Before connecting an item to a [project](../Projects/_projects.md), [resource map](../Atlas/Maps/Resources.md), or durable [note](../Atlas/Notes/_notes.md), review its provenance, rights, sensitivity, and destination with a human. You can leave it here while those questions remain open.

This page describes the folder; it is not an inbox item. The view lists the 10 newest inbox items by file creation time.
