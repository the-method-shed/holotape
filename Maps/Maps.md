# Maps

Maps are curated routes through related work and ideas, not categories every page must fit into. Use them to **gather** links to relevant pages, **develop** connections or questions between them, and **navigate** back to the underlying pages and their sources. A page can appear on several maps without being moved or copied. Unlike the [index](../index.md), a map is selective rather than a catalogue of everything.

Start with the maps that are useful now:

- [Resources](Resources.md) — reusable methods, references, and draft wiki guidance.
- [People](People.md) — professional profile stubs linked from projects; roles unconfirmed.
- [Projects](../Projects/_projects.md) — a landing page that also routes through time-bounded work.

When several reviewed notes share a question or responsibility, add links and a short explanation to an existing map or create a focused map for that subject. Don't pre-create empty area maps or folders. Keep unreviewed material in the [+ inbox](../+/_inbox.md) and catalogue it in the [index](../index.md); only link it from a curated map after its provenance, sensitivity, and destination have been reviewed with a human. For notes without a useful map yet, browse [Notes](../Notes/_notes.md) or the index.
